# AuraScribe

Official website: https://kikyo2006.github.io/aurascribe/
Project repository: https://github.com/kikyo2006/aurascribe

AuraScribe is a local-first transcription app for audio and video.

Key pages:
- Home: https://kikyo2006.github.io/aurascribe/
- Features: https://kikyo2006.github.io/aurascribe/features
- Documentation: https://kikyo2006.github.io/aurascribe/docs
- Privacy Policy: https://kikyo2006.github.io/aurascribe/privacy_policy.pdf

Key capabilities:
- Audio and video transcription
- URL transcription
- Speaker diarization
- AI assistant summaries
- Multiple export formats
- Multilingual support
- Works on Windows, macOS, and Linux
